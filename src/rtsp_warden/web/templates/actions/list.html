Editing actions

Actions are edited in config.yaml under actions:. Each camera rule lists the actions it triggers by name. Restart rtsp-warden after changing either.

actions:
  - name: phone
    type: ntfy
    url: https://ntfy.sh
    topic: ${NTFY_TOPIC}
    token: ${NTFY_TOKEN}
